Privacy policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

Your goals and reflections are stored on your device. Goals does not require an account or upload these entries to a developer-operated server.

Goals, reflections, and settings

Goals saves your daily and long-term goals, progress, reflections, history, reminder settings, and unsent feedback drafts locally. The app shares daily goal data with its Home Screen widget so you can view and complete goals there.

Goals has no built-in cloud sync. Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in backups managed by Apple. The developer does not have access to those backups.

Reminders

If you enable morning reminders, Goals asks for notification permission and schedules reminders on your device. You can turn them off in Goals or in iPhone Settings.

Feedback and support

Sending feedback is optional. The app opens the Messages composer, and nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. Your message and the sender details provided by Messages, such as your phone number or Apple Account email, are then available to the developer. Your goals and reflections are not attached automatically.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address and whatever you include. Feedback and support messages are used to respond to you, investigate problems, and improve Goals. Avoid including sensitive information you do not want to share.

Messages and email are handled by their respective service providers under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and tracking

Goals contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or use your feedback for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local entries remain until removed through available app controls or until you delete the app. Removing a long-term goal can leave its completed result in History. Offloading the app preserves its data; deleting the app removes its local data. Copies in device backups are managed separately through your Apple backup settings.
